About the Project

This project explores the impact of Artificial Intelligence on society, the economy, and the world of work through interactive data visualizations.

Our goal is to make complex AI-related data easier to understand, compare, and explore. As the project develops, we will add visualizations that highlight trends, differences, and patterns in AI adoption.

How Robust Is AI Planning?

Do AI systems solve the same planning problems when meaningful names are replaced by unfamiliar labels? Compare two evaluation conditions across eight planning domains.

Standard names Obfuscated names

What do the planning domain names mean?
  • Blocksworld: Reorder stacks of blocks under movement rules.
  • Childsnack: Prepare and serve meals while satisfying constraints.
  • Floortile: Coordinate robots to paint floor tiles.
  • Miconic: Plan elevator pickups and passenger deliveries.
  • Rovers: Plan exploration, sampling, and data transmission.
  • Sokoban: Push boxes to targets without blocking the path.
  • Spanner: Collect tools and tighten nuts in the required order.
  • Transport: Move packages between locations using vehicles.

Each domain has 45 benchmark tasks per condition. LAMA is a classical planning algorithm, not a language model. These are controlled planning tasks, not real-world employment outcomes. Sources: Stanford AI Index 2026, Figures 2.4.9–2.4.10; original planning study.
