Data Visualization Project | University of Genoa

Team: Yali Gamliel, Mishelle Zolotoy, Boaz Benush

About the Project

This project explores the impact of Artificial Intelligence on society, the economy, and the world of work through interactive data visualizations.

Our goal is to make complex AI-related data easier to understand, compare, and explore. As the project develops, we will add visualizations that highlight trends, differences, and patterns in AI adoption.

Data Sources

We plan to explore publicly available datasets from sources such as:

  • Stanford AI Index
  • Our World in Data
  • OECD AI
